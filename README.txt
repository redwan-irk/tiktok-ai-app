TikTok app legal pages

Files:
- terms.html: Terms of Service
- privacy.html: Privacy Policy

Before publishing:
1. Replace [APP NAME] in both HTML files.
2. Replace [YOUR CONTACT EMAIL] with an email address you can access.
3. Review the text so it accurately describes your actual app and data practices.
4. Publish both files publicly (for example, with GitHub Pages).
5. Enter the resulting public URLs in TikTok for Developers.

These are starter templates, not legal advice. Do not claim practices that your app does not actually follow.
